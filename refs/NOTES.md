# Style notes from Gangstar Vegas reference stills (for original recreation only; no assets copied)
- Night: deep navy/black sky with stars; teal haze near horizon; dusk variants teal->orange gradient.
- Warm sodium street lamps with big soft halos; pools of orange light on asphalt; cyan/magenta/green neon on towers.
- Glass towers: tall boxes with blue/teal emissive window grids, glowing neon sign bands, rooftop accents.
- Palm trees (tall, curved trunks, fan fronds) lining streets, lit green from below.
- Roads: dark asphalt, bold white dashed lines, yellow double lines, chunky crosswalks, raised curbs and tiled sidewalks.
- Cars: saturated colours (red/yellow/green), glossy, visible headlights/taillights, wheels with light rims; taxi yellow; police black-white with light bar.
- Character: chunky stylised proportions, hoodie/jacket, belt, boots, weapon held out.
- HUD: round minimap top-left with a round pause button above/left of it and ring bars; small round item buttons (medkit, armor) under minimap;
  weapon icon + ammo (e.g. 23/149) top-right; left virtual stick (ring with glowing knob); bottom-right round translucent buttons: aim/shoot (crosshair), run (sprinting figure), grenade/extra, gas/brake pedals when driving; translucent grey circles with white icons.
- Distant hills/rock silhouettes at horizon, bloom/glow on light sources, soft warm vs cool colour contrast.
